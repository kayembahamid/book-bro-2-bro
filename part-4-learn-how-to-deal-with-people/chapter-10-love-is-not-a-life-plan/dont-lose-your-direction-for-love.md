# Don’t Lose Your Direction For Love

Changing your whole life for love can feel romantic at first. Like proof. Proof that you care. Proof that you're committed. Proof that the person matters.

But not every sacrifice is love. Sometimes it's the beginning of losing yourself.

Plans change. Goals get delayed. Studies become less important. Friendships become quieter. Standards become negotiable.

And at the time, it can feel normal. Even beautiful. Because you're trying to show someone they matter.

But life can become painful when the relationship becomes the whole plan.

Relationships can change. People change. Feelings change. Circumstances change. Sometimes love grows stronger. Sometimes it ends.

And when it ends, some people don't just lose the relationship. They realize they also lost the direction they once had for themselves.

I've watched someone move cities, drop a course, and let a whole plan go quiet for a relationship, then have to rebuild all of it alone when it ended.

That's a hard place to be.

Love should influence your decisions. Of course it should. The person you care about will affect your choices. Where you live. How you spend your time. What you prioritize. What you sacrifice.

But there's a difference between love influencing your direction and love replacing it. One is connection. The other is disappearance.

You still need your own foundation. Your goals. Your values. Your standards. Your life. Not because you love the other person less. Because a relationship is too heavy when one person becomes the entire future.

A healthy relationship doesn't require one person to abandon themselves so the relationship can survive. It should be two people walking in the same direction while still knowing who they are. Supporting each other. Encouraging each other. Challenging each other. Making space for each other.

But neither person should have to disappear.

Love works best when it adds to your life. Not when it becomes your entire life.

Build your future around your values. Your character. Your purpose. Your direction. Then share that future with someone who respects it.

Because people can leave. Circumstances can change. Relationships can end. But the person who has to live with your choices is still you.

And that's why love shouldn't replace your direction.
