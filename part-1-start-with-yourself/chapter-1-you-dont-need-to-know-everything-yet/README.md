# Chapter 1 : You Don’t Need To Know Everything Yet

### You Don't Need To Know Everything Yet (chapter intro)

"Ham, can I ask you something?"

I was sitting at my desk trying to finish my lesson notes so I could finally go to lunch.

Everyone else had already left.

One student was still standing there.

"Sure," I said.

He pulled up a chair.

Then he asked a question I've probably heard a hundred times over the years.

"Ham, what am I supposed to do after school?"

At first, I thought he meant after graduation. University. A job. Something like that.

But the more we talked, the more I realized he wasn't really asking about school.

He was asking about life.

What should I do?

What if I choose the wrong thing?

What if everyone else figures it out before me?

What if I mess it all up?

I've heard versions of those questions from students for years.

Some ask it directly. Others hide it behind different questions. But underneath, it's usually the same fear.

"Ham, what am I supposed to do now?"

And honestly?

I don't think school prepares us for that question.

School teaches you how to pass tests. How to write essays. How to solve equations.

But nobody really sits you down and explains how to build a life.

You hear things like:

"Work hard."

"Get good grades."

"Be successful."

It sounds simple.

But successful according to who? Your parents? Your friends? Social media? The person online making their life look perfect?

A lot of us spend years chasing somebody else's definition of success.

Then one day, we realize we're living a life we never actually chose.

That's why direction matters.

Not because you need to know every step. You won't.

Not because you need a perfect plan. You don't.

But because life gets a little easier when you know what kind of person you're trying to become. What kind of life you're trying to build. What kind of future you're willing to work toward.

I'm not going to tell you exactly what to do.

I can't. Nobody can.

But I can help you think. About direction. About choices. About the pressure to figure everything out too early.

Because you don't need to know everything yet.

You just need to start paying attention to where you're going.

And trust me. Almost nobody has it all figured out.
