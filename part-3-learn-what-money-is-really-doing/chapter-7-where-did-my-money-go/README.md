# Chapter 7 :  Where Did My Money Go?

### Where Did My Money Go?&#x20;

The first salary feels bigger before you start spending it.

Then the month moves. Food. Coffee. Taxi rides. Eating out. Small things you barely notice.

And one day you check your account and think: "Wait. Where did it go?"

I remember one of my first full-time jobs. Getting paid felt great. For the first time, money was coming in regularly.

A month later, I checked my account and had one question. "Where did my money go?"

I wasn't broke. But I definitely had less money than I thought I should.

So I started thinking about where it had gone. Food, coffee, taxi rides, eating out, small purchases. Nothing felt expensive.

That's what surprised me. I wasn't losing money in one big decision. I was losing it in dozens of small ones.

At school, we spend years learning mathematics. But very little time learning how money actually works.

Then one day school ends. Money starts flowing in and out of your life. Rent. Bills. Taxes. Responsibilities. And somehow you're expected to know what you're doing.

I certainly didn't. I knew how much I earned. I had no idea how much I spent.

Looking back, that was probably the first real money lesson I learned.

Making money is one skill. Knowing where it goes is another.
