Language

Read this in the language you understand best

Every note on this page is real text, so your phone or computer can translate all of it. It takes about ten seconds and you only do it once.

iPhone

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the aA button in the address bar.
  3. Tap Translate Website.
  4. Choose your language.

Android

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three dots in the corner.
  3. Tap Translate.
  4. Choose your language.

Computer

  1. Right click anywhere on the page.
  2. Choose Translate to...
  3. Pick your language.

The goal is not to struggle through English. The goal is to understand the lessons.
