# Stop Waiting Until You Feel Ready

"What experience do you have?"

That question scares a lot of young people. They hear it and immediately think: Nothing. No real job. No company. No official title. No serious experience.

But then you start asking better questions.

Have you ever worked on a school project? Helped organize something? Taken care of younger siblings? Solved a problem at home? Worked with other people?

Suddenly, the answer isn't nothing anymore.

Every year, I hear some version of the same thing. "Ham, I'm not ready." Not ready to apply. Not ready to start. Not ready to put myself out there.

But when I ask a few questions, the conversation usually changes.

Because experience doesn't only come from a job. Experience comes from doing things. Solving problems. Helping people. Taking responsibility. Working with others. Trying something before you feel confident.

Life leaves clues about what you're capable of. The mistake is ignoring them.

A lot of us keep waiting until we feel ready. Ready to apply. Ready to start. Ready to take a chance.

But you may never feel completely ready.

Experience comes after trying. Confidence comes after action.

You move forward by starting before you feel prepared.

Because waiting doesn't create experience. Doing does.
