# Failure Is Information

Failure hurts because it feels personal.

A rejected application feels like rejection of you. A failed exam feels like proof you're not smart. A mistake feels like evidence you weren't ready.

You fail an exam. Get turned down for a job. Send something out and hear nothing back. Start something and it doesn't work.

And the first thought is always: maybe I'm not good enough.

I've had that thought. Then it slowly changed.

Failure doesn't tell you who you are. It tells you what happened.

The failed interview shows you something. The rejected application is feedback. The idea that didn't work points at what you missed.

The only question is whether you're willing to look.

Every setback carries information. What worked? What didn't? What would I do differently?

Failure is feedback.

The only question is whether you'll read it.
