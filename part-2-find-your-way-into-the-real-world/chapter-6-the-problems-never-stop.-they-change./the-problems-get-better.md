# The Problems Get Better

If problems never really stop, then maybe zero problems was never the goal.

What if the goal is having better ones?

Problems that come from growth. From responsibility. From building something. From becoming someone stronger than you used to be.

That took me a long time to understand.

When you're young, it's easy to think successful people have fewer problems. The older you get, the less true that feels.

No money comes with money problems. Money comes with different problems. Looking for a job comes with job problems. Having a job comes with workplace problems. Managers have people problems. Business owners have business problems. Parents have parent problems.

The problems don't disappear. They change.

For a long time, I thought solving all my problems was the goal. Now I think the goal is learning which problems are worth carrying.

Some problems come from staying stuck. Some come from avoiding responsibility. Some come from refusing to grow.

But some problems come because your life is expanding. You're learning more. Carrying more. Building more. Becoming responsible for more than just yourself.

When I look back, some of the hardest periods of my life were also the periods where I learned the most. Not because they were easy. Because they forced me to adapt.

Problems are usually a sign that something is changing.

The goal is becoming the kind of person who can handle bigger challenges.

Because success doesn't remove problems. It usually replaces them with better ones.
