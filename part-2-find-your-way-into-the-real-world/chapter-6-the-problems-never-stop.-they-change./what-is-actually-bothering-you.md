# What Is Actually Bothering You?

Your feelings tell the truth. Just not the whole truth.

They tell you something's wrong. They make you pay attention. They don't always point at the real thing first.

I learned that in one of my first sales jobs.

At the time I thought: I hate this job.

That felt like the problem. The job.

But when I slowed down I noticed I didn't hate all of it. I didn't hate talking to people. I didn't hate learning sales. I didn't even mind working hard.

I hated the pressure. The rejection. The feeling of not knowing what I was doing.

That's a different problem. The job wasn't it. The real thing was hiding underneath the feeling.

Someone says "I hate studying." Maybe they hate one subject.

Someone says "my boss hates me." Maybe the communication is bad.

Someone says "I'm terrible with money." Maybe they never made a plan.

Feelings are useful. They tell you something's wrong. They don't tell you what.

So before a big decision, slow down and ask: what is actually bothering me?

Remember the student who wanted to quit university? Same lesson, smaller scale. Name the real thing before you act on it.

Because if you solve the wrong problem, you can change everything and still feel stuck.
