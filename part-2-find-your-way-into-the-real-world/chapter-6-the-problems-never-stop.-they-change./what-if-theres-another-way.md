# What If There’s Another Way?

"What if there's another way?"

That question has saved me more time than trying harder ever has.

Because when something isn't working, my first instinct is usually to push. More effort. More time. More pressure.

But sometimes more isn't the answer.

I once spent hours trying to solve something the same way. Again. And again. And again. Nothing changed.

Then I searched online and found someone doing it in a completely different way. Ten minutes later, the whole thing was fixed.

That annoyed me. But it also taught me something. The answer isn't always to push harder. Sometimes it's to step back and ask a better question.

I've seen this everywhere. A student keeps using a study method that isn't helping. Someone at work keeps making the same mistake. A couple keeps having the same argument. A person keeps trying to fix a money issue with the same bad habit.

Different situations. Same pattern.

They're not always missing effort. They may be missing a different approach. A different method. A different person to explain it. A different angle. A different way to look at the situation.

That doesn't mean you're lazy. It means you're thinking.

And thinking can save you from spending months pushing against the same wall.

So before you keep pushing, pause and ask yourself: "What if there's another way?"

Because sometimes the best solution looks strange until it works.
