# Pay Attention To How Things Work

#### I Didn't Know That Was A Rule

Being new at work is strange.

You're trying to do the job. But you're also trying to understand the room. Who needs to be updated? Who makes decisions? What matters here? What annoys people? What do people expect without saying?

No one tells you everything. You have to notice.

That was one of the first things work taught me.

School and work are different. In school, most of the rules are explained. A teacher gives instructions. A timetable tells you where to be. A test tells you what chapter matters.

At work, some rules are spoken. Others are just expected. You learn them by watching. By asking. By paying attention to what happens when something goes wrong.

Sometimes the mistake isn't that you're lazy. Or careless. Or bad at the job. Sometimes you simply didn't know what mattered yet.

But once you notice it, you're responsible for learning. Who should I tell before I leave? Who needs an update? What should I ask before I decide? What do people here care about? What makes the work easier for everyone else?

That's the part many young people miss at first. Doing the job is one thing. Learning how the workplace works is another.

The people who grow fastest are the ones paying attention.

Looking back, my first job wasn't only teaching me tasks. It was teaching me how to notice things.
