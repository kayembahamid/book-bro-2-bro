# Who Are You Becoming?

There's one person you take into every room. Every job. Every relationship. Every success. Every mistake. Every private decision no one else sees.

You.

Which is why who you're becoming matters more than most people notice.

Plenty of questions follow you through life. What do you want to study? What job? How much do you want to earn? Where do you want to live?

They matter. They're not the whole question.

Careers change. Money comes and goes. Relationships begin and end. Plans work and fail. Through all of it, one person is still there.

You.

So the question isn't only what do I want. It's who am I becoming while I chase it.

You can get exactly what you wanted and not like who you became to get it. You can earn more and lose your peace. Build a career and lose your character. Be admired by everyone and still feel uncomfortable alone with yourself.

Your habits. Your honesty. Your discipline. Your patience. Your kindness. Your ability to keep your word when no one is watching.

None of that looks impressive early on. It doesn't get applause. Over time it becomes the foundation everything else stands on.

Because the person you become is the one who has to live with your choices. Not for a day. Not for a season. For the rest of it.

So don't only ask what you want to have. Ask what kind of person you're turning into.

That question is heavier than the others, because it doesn't measure success. It measures the person success left behind.

Character.
